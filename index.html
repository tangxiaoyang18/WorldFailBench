01 / OVERVIEW

Failure is more
than a binary label.

Robot policies are increasingly evaluated inside generated worlds. But matching the final outcome does not establish that a model reproduces the underlying failure.

WorldFailBench compares closed-loop policy rollouts in world models with reference execution in simulators and on a physical robot. It examines observable errors, the stages where execution breaks down, and the scene changes that turn success into failure.

The benchmark spans LIBERO, RoboTwin, and the G1 humanoid. Experiments reveal diagnostic gaps that aggregate success rates can hide.

Move from a single outcome to an interpretable account of policy behavior.

A consistent denominator. Failure-type and failure-stage frequencies are calculated over all evaluation episodes, including successes and indeterminate failures. Labels describe observable behavior rather than hidden policy causes.
